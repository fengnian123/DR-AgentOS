\paragraph{Generalization and physical deployment.}
Across five simulators, ours leads seven of ten metrics (\cref{tab:domains}).
On 64 matched M20 trials, \cref{tab:real-main} improves both success and
efficiency; details are in \appref{app:real-world-eval}.

Across these evaluations, the gains appear at three levels: cross-benchmark
results improve initial destination selection, N4 isolates recovery under
execution-time motion, and robot trials translate the same belief interface
into shorter paths and fewer inspections. Their agreement argues against the
gain coming from benchmark-specific exploration or a stronger controller alone.

\subsection{Ablation Studies}
\label{sec:ablations}

\paragraph{Core components.}
\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{figures/core_component_ablation_grouped.pdf}
    \caption{Core component ablation; error bars show five-seed standard deviations.}
    \label{fig:module_ablation}
\end{figure}

The full agent reaches 62.17 First-Inspection SR, 85.33 Search SR, and 70.87
SPL with 3.95 inspections (\cref{fig:module_ablation}). Without predictive
belief, First-Inspection SR falls to 12.51; Latest Observation Only loses 20.91
Search SR points, showing that temporally persistent history guides both the
initial destination and recovery. Direct candidate prediction loses 13.84/15.75
First-Inspection/Search points, supporting the persistence--relocation
factorization. Top-1-only search loses 22.41 Search SR points, while removing
evidence updates loses 42.02 points and raises inspections to 6.48. Thus the
gain comes from retaining alternatives and revising them online, not from a
single stronger guess or a larger search budget.
The pattern separates the roles of the components: predictive belief chooses
where inspection begins, the full distribution preserves fallback hypotheses,
and evidence updating converts new views into route changes. Because No
Evidence Update uses the same predictor, its gap isolates the action-time
filter rather than improved offline state classification.
