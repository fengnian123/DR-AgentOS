\evolvingworldnav{} consistently improves the evaluated metrics across FindingDory,
GOAT-Bench, and \pfdbench{}. Across \pfdbench{} N1--N5, it improves First-Inspection SR from
45.33\% (DynaMem) to 61.32\% and Search SR from 81.00\% to 86.18\%,
showing that arrival-time prediction and en-route evidence improve the first
fully covered inspection while retained hypotheses support recovery. The gains on FindingDory
and GOAT-Bench further show that predictive memory remains effective beyond
the proposed evolving-world setting.
