\section{Results and Analysis}
\label{sec:results}

This working draft fixes the complete reporting structure without fabricating
unfinished measurements. Every \na{} must be replaced by a reproduced value or
a clearly marked published result before submission. The tables are organized to
test distinct claims rather than repeat one aggregate score: breadth across
benchmarks, effectiveness on the proposed problem, domain generalization,
mechanistic contribution, model dependence, and physical deployment.

selected a route, whether the target should have been visible, how the posterior
changed, and which action followed. Failures will be categorized as entity
association, missing candidate, stale-state prediction, overconfident relocation,
insufficient visibility, detector error, localization drift, unreachable
viewpoint, or replanning error.
