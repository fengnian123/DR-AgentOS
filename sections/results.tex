\subsection{Main Results}
\label{sec:results}

\paragraph{Benchmark comparison.}
We first ask whether predictive memory transfers across long-horizon,
repeated-goal, and evolving-world tasks. Across FindingDory, GOAT-Bench, and
\pfdbench{}, \evolvingworldnav{} leads all headline metrics; on \pfdbench{},
First-Go and Search SR exceed the strongest baselines by 15.99 and 5.18 points.
The larger first-attempt gain shows that prediction improves the initial
destination, while the remaining Search-SR gain reflects recovery from retained
alternatives. Full cross-benchmark and search-cost results are reported in
\cref{tab:crossbench,tab:mainnav}.

\paragraph{Qualitative mechanism.}
\Cref{fig:p4d_case_study} traces a routine-world episode in which a bowl moves
from its last-seen cabinet to the sink during dinner cleanup. The predictive
belief ranks the current state first, whereas stale or geometry-only orderings
spend an inspection on an obsolete location.

\paragraph{Generalization and physical deployment.}
Cross-environment results span MP3D, HM3D, Habitat-GS, InteriorGS, and HSSD
without pooling incompatible task definitions; \evolvingworldnav{} leads seven

SAGE-Bench SPL (\cref{tab:domains}). We then evaluate 64 matched LYNX M20
search trials per method, balanced across indoor and outdoor environments, with

\subsection{Ablation Studies}
\label{sec:ablations}

\paragraph{Core components.}
We separately remove predictive history, persistence--relocation factorization,
distributional search, and online evidence updating while keeping perception
and control fixed.

\begin{figure}[!t]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{figures/core_component_ablation.pdf}
    \caption{Core component ablation; error bars show five-seed standard deviations.}
    \label{fig:module_ablation}
\end{figure}

\Cref{fig:module_ablation} shows distinct contributions: direct candidate
prediction and Top-1-only search reduce Search SR by 15.75 and 22.41 points,
while removing evidence updates causes the largest degradation. Thus, the gain
requires both a predictive distribution and the observe--update--replan loop.
