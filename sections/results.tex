\paragraph{Cross-benchmark comparison.}
\Cref{tab:crossbench} shows gains across all three benchmarks (\na{} denotes
unavailable measurements). On \pfdbench{}, \evolvingworldnav{} achieves
61.32\% First-Go SR and 86.18\% Search SR, exceeding DynaMem by 15.99 and
5.18 points. The larger initial-destination gain supports predicting the current
state before collecting new evidence. Complementary recovery and search-cost
results appear in \cref{tab:mainnav}; cross-environment results are retained
in \cref{tab:domains}.

\paragraph{Physical deployment.}
\label{sec:real-world-results}
We run 64 matched LYNX M20 trials per method, balanced across indoor and
outdoor settings, with three inspections and 360 seconds per trial. Shared
histories, candidates, perception, and navigation isolate the memory pathway.
In \cref{tab:real-main}, Search SR rises from 37.5\% to 48.4\% over
Retrieval + Reasoning, while travel falls from 50.3\,m to 43.8\,m.
Recovery remains 24.3\%; detailed breakdowns are in \cref{app:real-world}.

\paragraph{Core components.}
With perception and control fixed, direct candidate prediction loses
15.75 Search-SR points, retaining only Top-1 loses 22.41 points, and removing
evidence updates reduces Search SR to 43.31\%
(\cref{fig:module_ablation}). These controls support separate contributions
from factorized prediction, alternative hypotheses, and online belief revision.
Fine-grained controls are in \cref{app:fine-grained-ablation}.

\paragraph{When does prediction help?}
With scenes and queries fixed, \pfdnav{} gains 22.36 routine-world SR points
over Last Seen and 12.03 over Transformer Direct (\cref{tab:worlds}). It trails
Last Seen in both static and random worlds, bounding the benefit to learnable
temporal structure. Staleness and budget diagnostics are in
\cref{fig:predictive-memory}.

\paragraph{Current-state prediction.}
\label{sec:prediction-results}
Temporal queries exclude unchanged states, whereas LOSO queries balance changed
and unchanged states in held-out scenes; their absolute accuracies are not
directly comparable. Ours improves Top-1 over the strongest shared semantic
prior by 2.22 and 1.71 points, respectively, while also improving MRR and NLL
(\cref{tab:predictors}). The full predictor comparison and calibration analysis
are in \cref{app:full-prediction-results}. Across five frozen VLMs, mean
Search-SR gains are 20.90 points (\cref{tab:foundation_models}).
