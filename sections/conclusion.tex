\section{Conclusion}

We study persistent navigation when task-relevant state can change outside the
agent's view before or during execution. \evolvingworldnav{} converts
timestamped 4D histories into a persistence--relocation belief, plans under its
uncertainty, and revises it with visibility-aware evidence. Across \pfdbench{},
external benchmarks, and real robots, this closed loop improves initial
destination selection and recovery; paired controls show its clearest advantage
under learnable temporal structure rather than arbitrary motion. Future work
will extend the belief model to interacting objects and continuously changing
goals.
