\section{Conclusion}

We study persistent navigation under unobserved changes before and during
execution. \evolvingworldnav{} couples a persistence--relocation belief from 4D
histories with an event-driven predict--observe--replan filter. Across
\pfdbench{}, external benchmarks, and robots, it improves first-inspection
selection and recovery, especially under learnable temporal structure. Future
work will address interacting objects and continuously changing goals.
