\section{Limitations}

Although \dragentos{} is motivated by general task-relevant world evolution, the current predictive model is operationalized primarily through discrete portable-object states; structural reconfiguration and changing traversability require richer state and action models. \pfdnav{} also assumes reliable entity association and an available set of candidate receptacles and inspection viewpoints; pose drift, missed detections, identity switches, or missing candidates can corrupt both prediction and navigation. Its discrete belief represents arbitrary continuous placements less directly than continuous generative models. The source datasets are complementary rather than jointly observed, so \pfdbench{} episodes are human-trace-grounded simulations, not reconstructions of one household. The main protocol freezes the target after the query; N4 is required to test simultaneous target and agent motion. Finally, visibility-aware updates reduce weak negative evidence but cannot guarantee recovery when beliefs and perception fail together or routines shift.
