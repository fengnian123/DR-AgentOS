\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Embodied navigation and agent control.}
Object-goal, language-conditioned, and exploratory navigation couple semantic
perception with spatial reasoning and action
\citep{anderson2018evaluation,savva2019habitat,chaplot2020objectnav,
xin2026agentvln}. Open-vocabulary maps and scene graphs ground language in
metric or structured space \citep{huang2023vlmaps,gu2024conceptgraphs,
werby2024hovsg,Li_2026_CVPR,huang2026msgnav}, while recent agents compose frozen foundation models with
maps, tools, and reusable navigation skills
\citep{yokoyama2024vlfm,long2024instructnav,ziliotto2025tango,
li2026agenticnav}. Active-search methods further use semantic relations or
belief maps to order exploration \citep{zheng2021cos,ginting2024seek,
zhang2025apexnav}. These approaches mainly assume that the task-relevant state
remains compatible with the agent's map during execution. Our focus is the
complementary case in which stored evidence is historically correct but may
become stale before or during navigation because changes occur outside the
current view.

\paragraph{Persistent memory and changing worlds.}
Long-horizon benchmarks require agents to retain experience across visits or
tasks \citep{khanna2024goatbench,majumdar2024openeqa,
yadav2025findingdory}, and open-vocabulary 3D/4D memories support spatial and
temporal retrieval \citep{liu2024dynamem,sohn2025r4,gorlo2026describe}.
Dynamic mapping typically filters moving content or updates a representation
after a change is observed \citep{bescos2018dynaslam,narayana2020lifelong}.
Scene Graph Memory and PredictiveGraphs instead model evolving object--location
relations \citep{kurenkov2023dynamic,saavedraruiz2026predictivegraphs}, while
FlowMaps predicts multimodal future placements for dynamic ObjectNav
\citep{argenziano2026flowmaps}. Our contribution is not prediction alone: we
turn persistent identity and temporal provenance into an action-facing belief
about the latent present. The belief separates persistence from relocation,
retains alternatives for search, and is repeatedly tested with
visibility-qualified evidence in an observe--update--replan loop.

\paragraph{Human routines and hidden world evolution.}
Household activities contain temporal and person-specific regularities.
HOMER/HOMER+ model object-use routines, STREAK studies continual adaptation,
and personalized navigation systems exploit user habits
\citep{patel2023homer,patel2023routine,bartoli2025streak,zhang2026ucon}.
Egocentric and reconstructed-home datasets provide complementary traces of
human--object interaction \citep{perrett2025hdepic,kim2025parahome}.
\pfdbench{} converts such traces into executable, causally bounded scene
histories and pairs static, routine, and random worlds while holding scene and
query factors fixed. This design distinguishes prediction of unobserved change
from online target tracking, post-change map repair, and frequency-based
search, positioning \evolvingworldnav{} at the intersection of long-term
memory, temporal prediction, active perception, and embodied planning.
