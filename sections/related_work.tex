\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Embodied navigation and agent control.}
Embodied navigation combines semantic perception, spatial reasoning, and action,
while open-vocabulary maps and scene graphs ground language goals in metric or
structured space
\citep{anderson2018evaluation,savva2019habitat,chaplot2020objectnav,
xin2026agentvln,huang2023vlmaps,gu2024conceptgraphs,werby2024hovsg,
Li_2026_CVPR,huang2026msgnav}. Foundation-model agents combine maps, tools, and
navigation skills
\citep{yokoyama2024vlfm,long2024instructnav,ziliotto2025tango,
li2026agenticnav,zheng2021cos,ginting2024seek,zhang2025apexnav}. We study
navigation when past observations become stale through unobserved changes
before or during execution.

\paragraph{Persistent memory and changing worlds.}
Memory benchmarks evaluate navigation across tasks
\citep{khanna2024goatbench,yadav2025findingdory} and episodic question answering
\citep{majumdar2024openeqa}; 4D memories support spatiotemporal retrieval
\citep{sohn2025r4,gorlo2026describe}. Dynamic mapping filters moving content or
updates maps from observations
\citep{bescos2018dynaslam,narayana2020lifelong,liu2024dynamem}. Predictive
navigation infers locations from partial histories, temporal filters, or
multimodal continuous placements
\citep{kurenkov2023dynamic,saavedraruiz2026predictivegraphs,
argenziano2026flowmaps}. PredictiveGraphs already combines future-state
prediction, Top-$k$ navigation, online observations, and agent tools. Our focus
is complementary: a stepwise filtering formulation for continued hidden
evolution during execution, in which a posterior is propagated to arrival
times, updated once from newly visible evidence, and allowed to reopen earlier
hypotheses. The belief also separates persistence from relocation and retains
executable mass outside the mapped candidate set.

\paragraph{Human routines and hidden world evolution.}
HOMER/HOMER+ capture household object-use routines, STREAK studies continual
adaptation across household contexts, and personalized navigation exploits user
habits
\citep{patel2023homer,patel2023routine,bartoli2025streak,zhang2026ucon}.
HD-EPIC and ParaHome provide human--object interaction traces
\citep{perrett2025hdepic,kim2025parahome}. \pfdbench{} uses human traces and
separately tracked simulated routine evidence to constrain executable scene
histories while exposing only observations available by the query time.
Its paired static, routine, and random worlds control scene and query factors
to test whether temporal regularities improve search beyond location frequencies.
